# BRIEF — "Follow one request" (Scrollcraft step 0)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner asked for "the best plan … scan
our features and workflow to bring this to life", then "make it, so we can see it". Earlier
direction, quoted: "someone looking for help, and their request being followed into the scroll",
"show what a developer can do … with AI safely", "for anyone that wants to use it", and on the
rejected previs, "it's dark and hard to follow".

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** calm, precise, trustworthy, alive. References outside the web: an air-traffic
   control strip board, a lab notebook, a courtroom exhibit table.
2. **Journey (owner's words, ordered):** someone looking for help → their request followed
   through the pipeline → sorted and flagged → a developer uses AI safely → the fix is proven →
   it stays fixed.
3. **Energy:** quiet open, rising through the board, a held breath at the AI dial, the loudest
   moment at the proof, then calm.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the curve below. The moment: "the test failed, then it
   passed, and that's the proof."
5. **What no other site does:** the visitor sets what an AI is allowed to see and watches the
   customer's data stay locked out. (Signature move.)
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, high-key. Nothing dark.
7. **World:** distinct states of one product surface. Not a camera flight.
8. **Assets:** real product vocabulary and the committed demo evidence bundle
   (`web/src/lib/demo-bundle.json`, produced by `scripts/demo_red_to_green.py`). No generated
   imagery.

## Decisions

- **Grammar: live surface.** The page behaves like the product. Copy lives in the surface's idiom:
  a guide panel, status lines, tab labels. The other seven lost: filmic one-shot and continuous
  world are the rejected camera flight; poster and gallery cannot show a workflow; chaptered
  editorial and cutlist tell rather than show; split stage is borrowed only for the phone layout.
- **The followed object:** the report chip in the surface header. It gains one badge per beat.
- **Two depths:** the console's own switch, "Show technical detail", and its own label pairs.
- **One source of truth:** every number, label and artifact on screen is read from the demo
  bundle at build time; a test fails if the page and the bundle disagree.

## Journey and feeling curve

| Beat | What changes on the surface | Feeling | Caused by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Transfer form; Review transfer; HTTP 500; the customer types their report | mild unease | a real error state, in their words |
| Turn | What left the browser: six structural steps; the typed message scrubbed field by field; 9 fields removed | relief | private details visibly gone |
| Substance | The console board: lands in "Waiting for a test run", graded A (0.96), ticket drafts with priority | control | it's sorted, graded and drafted |
| Range (signature) | "What an AI sees": the share dial, the packet per scope, the locked never-list | trust | you set the limits |
| **Peak** | "Proof it's fixed": old code fails, the fix passes, confirmed fixed, measured | certainty | proof, not opinion |
| Commitment | The command line | readiness | one command away |

**The peak:** "It's the site where you watch the test fail, then pass, and that's the proof."
It lives in the proof beat and gets the largest scroll span; the AI beat before it is quieter.

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where you follow one bug report all the way to a
proven fix, and you decide what the AI gets to see."

**Authored silence:** the first 15% of the proof beat holds still on purpose (the breath before
the peak). It is not dead scroll.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | tilt-to-flat + kinetic typing in the reporter | the surface "wakes"; their words appear |
| Turn | focus zoom + strike-through reveal | the eye goes where the data is removed |
| Substance | count + slide-in | the board populates like a real queue |
| Range | pointer (the dial) + rack between columns | the visitor operates it |
| Peak | push-in + pin | stillness, then the result |
| Commitment | settle + real input | the page stops moving and offers a command |

Six device families, none repeated back to back, no scrubbed video.
